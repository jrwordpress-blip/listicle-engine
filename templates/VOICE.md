# Voice Context — The Blockopedia

## Pronoun stance
Third person for the publisher - no "we", no brand chest-beating. "You" for the reader when
giving a decision framework. Never "I". Author account is `The Blockopedia`.

## Lexical rules
- **Contractions**: partial - natural in prose, avoided in the Key Takeaways and table cells.
- **Sentence ceiling**: 30 words. Vary length hard; a 6-word sentence after a 28-word one.
- **Paragraph ceiling**: 75 words, 3 sentences max in vendor profiles.
- **Summary label**: "Key Takeaways"
- **Dashes**: spaced hyphen ( - ) only. Zero em dashes, zero en dashes in prose.

## Headline patterns
- **Favor**: buyer's query as the headline; a real number; the year; the outcome
  ("Best Crypto Consultant Companies & Firms to Know in 2026").
- **Avoid**: "Ultimate Guide", "Everything You Need to Know", fake urgency, clickbait,
  colons stacked with two promises.

## Voice fingerprint (0-1 scales)
- Funny vs serious: 0.15 (serious, dry)
- Formal vs casual: 0.35 (plain professional, not corporate)
- Respectful vs irreverent: 0.25 (respectful, but willing to call a red flag a red flag)
- Enthusiastic vs matter-of-fact: 0.8 (matter-of-fact)

## Readability target
- Audience tier: professional
- Flesch Grade: 9-12
- Read-aloud test: if a sentence sounds like a vendor's own homepage, rewrite it.

## Voice signature (non-negotiable)
- One opinionated judgment per article: state who each vendor is *wrong* for, not only who it
  suits.
- One honest admission of limitation per article: say plainly what the research could not
  verify (self-reported headcounts, undisclosed rates) instead of papering over it.
- Soft close only: segment the reader and hand them the next decision. No hard CTA, no
  "contact us today".
