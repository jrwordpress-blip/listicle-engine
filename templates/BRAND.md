## Audience
- **Primary**: Buyers evaluating a crypto/blockchain vendor - founders, CTOs and product leads
  who have already decided to build and now need a shortlist plus the reason for each name.
  Technically literate, commercially skeptical, comparing 3-5 tabs at once.
- **Secondary**: Enterprise and fintech buyers in regulated markets (US, EU) who need
  compliance-ready partners; and early-stage/SME buyers screening on budget.
- **Active problems**: "Hundreds of agencies all claim the same expertise - which ones are real?"
  Budget uncertainty, regulatory exposure, fear of picking a vendor that cannot ship or maintain.
- **Common misconceptions**: that hourly rate maps to quality; that a single-chain wallet is
  still viable; that white-label and custom builds carry the same risk; that a vendor's own
  marketing page is evidence.

  giving an opinionated shortlist instead of a filterable database.

## Editorial Rules
### Always do
- Open on a sourced market statistic with an outbound link, then name the reader in paragraph 2.
- 5-7 Key Takeaways, each a standalone claim a skimmer or an AI answer engine can lift.
- Give every list entry the same skeleton: h3 `N. Entity Name`, positioning prose, a detail
  table, "Other Services", and a "Why Choose" verdict naming the buyer profile it fits.
- Segment the conclusion by buyer type (enterprise -> X, startup -> Y).
- Name risk in its own section: fees, regulatory exposure, red flags.
- Concrete over adjectival: "250+ engineers, 60+ deployed solutions" beats "a leading provider".
- Reconcile every number against the comparison table before shipping - one figure per fact.

### Never do
- Never state a vendor's founding year, headcount, rate or client count that is not sourced.
  If unverified, omit it or mark it for the VERIFY list. No filling gaps with plausible numbers.
- Never name a company in the conclusion or FAQ that is not in the list body.
- Never promise guaranteed returns, "100x", "risk-free", or "best in the world".
- Never use em dashes. The site uses a spaced hyphen ( - ) everywhere an em dash would go.
- Never hyperlink vendor websites inside the detail table - plain text only, no equity leak.
- Never dump a "related posts" block; internal links are full sentences that earn the link.
- Never hand-insert a table of contents (Easy Table of Contents generates it).

### Taboo phrases
game-changer, revolutionary, cutting-edge (as a standalone claim), unlock, delve, seamless,
robust ecosystem, in today's fast-paced world, look no further, the sky is the limit,
guaranteed returns, risk-free, 100x, best in the world, leading provider (unsourced)

### Required disclosures
- Pricing and cost ranges are indicative market ranges, not quotes: state that a custom quote
  is required.
- Anything touching investment or returns: general information, not financial advice.

## Topic Scope
- **In scope (core pillars)**: crypto/blockchain development vendors (wallet, smart contract,
  NFT, GameFi, Web3), exchange and listing services, crypto consulting firms, development cost
  guides, buyer comparison explainers.
- **Partial scope**: individual tokens, exchanges and tools - only as a buyer-comparison angle,
  never as a price call or a hold/sell recommendation.
- **Out of scope**: price prediction, trading signals, airdrop farming, anything requiring a
  personalized investment recommendation.
